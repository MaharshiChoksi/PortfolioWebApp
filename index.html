Who's Maharshi?

I'm a licensed Financial Services Agent based in Calgary (LLQP — Life Insurance, Segregated Funds & Annuities), helping individuals and families build a real financial foundation — not just a product sale.

My background is unconventional. I came through financial technology — building algorithmic trading systems, quantitative risk models, and automated market analysis tools. That background gives me one clear edge: I think in systems. Most people don't fail financially because of bad luck — they fail because they have no structure. I help fix that.

I also build and lead a team of agents in Calgary. If you're looking for a flexible career in financial services with real mentorship and no experience required — that's a conversation worth having.
